Rental Turn Process — Target: 1 Day

Industry median is 9 days. We beat it by 8. Every vacant day = lost revenue.

💰
The math: A room sitting empty 1 extra week at $175/week = $175 gone forever. Turn cost target: $100/room or less. Same-day relist after Jerel approves photos.
8-Step Turn Sequence
  1. 1
    Linen Inspection & Removal
    Strip all bedding. Inspect mattress for stains/damage/bugs. Check mattress cover. Stained through cover → replace cover ($15). Damaged mattress → flag for replacement. Bag linens for laundering.
    Andrea or Sandra
  2. 2
    Trash Removal
    Empty all bins: room, closet, bathroom. Check under bed, behind furniture, inside drawers. Replace all trash liners. Items left behind → bag and hold 48 hours, then dispose.
    Andrea or Sandra
  3. 3
    Dusting & Surfaces
    Dust all surfaces: shelves, mirrors, light fixtures, ceiling fan blades, window sills, baseboards, closet rod. Wipe all furniture with damp cloth then dry.
    Andrea or Sandra
  4. 4
    Floor Cleaning
    Vacuum/sweep hard floors. Mop with disinfectant. Check corners and under furniture. Wipe door frames, window trim. Note any floor damage.
    Andrea or Sandra
  5. 5
    High-Touch Sanitization
    Disinfect: light switches, door handles both sides, faucet handles, toilet flush handle, drawer pulls, closet rod, window locks. Hospital-grade wipes. Non-negotiable.
    Andrea or Sandra
  6. 6
    Bathroom Deep Clean
    Toilet: inside bowl, outside, seat top+bottom, base. Shower/tub: scrub walls, floor, grout, drain, showerhead. Sink+vanity: basin, faucet, handles, counter. Mirror: streak-free. Check caulking — re-caulk if peeling.
    Andrea or Sandra
  7. 7
    Amenity Restock + Visual Standard
    Fresh towels (1 bath, 1 hand). Fresh linens. Replace burned-out bulbs. Welcome kit: sheets, towels, laundry pod, dish soap, hand soap, 2 rolls TP. Make bed hotel-style per the Visual Standard section. Test smoke detector.
    Andrea or Sandra
  8. 8
    Final Inspection + 4 Photos → Jerel
    Walk room as if you're the new tenant. Test: lock code, lights, outlets, faucets (hot+cold), toilet, shower, window, closet, WiFi. Take 4 photos: (1) full room from doorway, (2) bed/closet, (3) bathroom, (4) lock keypad showing green. Text to Jerel. Room is NOT listed until photos approved.
    Andrea/Sandra → Jerel approves
⚡
The Rule: Same-day relist. Once Jerel approves turn photos, room gets listed that same day. No exceptions. Vacancy is permanent lost revenue.

House Rules — Posted in Every Property

Kitchen wall, next to the fridge, eye level. Laminated. Same spot, every house.

⚠️
These rules protect everyone — tenants AND the property. Enforce them every single time. One ignored violation signals to all tenants that rules are optional.
1
No Smoking Anywhere
No cigarettes, vaping, marijuana, incense, or candles inside the home or on the property. This is a fire risk and violates lease.
Violation → Immediate written notice. 2nd offense → eviction.
2
Kitchen Clean After Every Use
Wash dishes immediately or put in dishwasher. Wipe stove after cooking. Counters clear. Sink empty. No food left out.
Violation → Verbal warning. 3rd offense → written notice.
3
No Overnight Guests
Guests must leave by 9 PM. Only persons on the lease may sleep in the home. No exceptions.
Violation → Written notice. 2nd offense → eviction proceedings.
4
Fridge = One Shelf Per Tenant
Each tenant has one labeled shelf (their room number). No unlabeled food. Sunday = expired food purge day.
Violation → Verbal reminder. Unlabeled food may be discarded.
5
Quiet Hours: 10 PM – 7 AM
No loud music, TV, or noise after 10 PM. Respect your neighbors. This is a working household.
Violation → Verbal warning. Persistent violations → lease termination.
6
Common Areas Stay Common
No personal items stored in living room, hallways, or shared spaces. Remove laundry from washer/dryer within 1 hour.
Violation → Items may be removed by staff. Tenant notified same day.
Enforcement Ladder — Follow This Order Every Time
  1. 1
    Verbal Warning (Private)
    Pull tenant aside privately. "Hey [Name], I need to talk to you about something real quick." State what you saw. State the rule. "Can you handle that now?" Thank them. Log date + incident and text Jerel.
  2. 2
    Written Warning
    Second violation of same rule. Write it up. Tenant signs. File it. Jerel is notified. This is on record.
  3. 3
    Final Warning
    Third violation. Jerel calls the tenant directly. Clear message: next violation = end of lease. Documented.
  4. 4
    Non-Renewal / Eviction Proceedings
    Jerel decides. Follow Florida landlord-tenant law. Document everything from step 1 forward.
Violation Conversation Script — Word for Word

Use this script every time. Stay calm. Stay private.

"Hey [Name], I need to talk to you about something real quick."
[State what you saw:] "I noticed [dishes were left in the sink / someone was here overnight / I smelled smoke]."
"That's one of our house rules — [state the rule simply]."
"I need you to take care of that. Can you handle it now?"
[If they push back:] "I hear you, but the rule is the same for everyone. I just need you to handle it."
"Thanks, I appreciate it."

Then: Write down date + tenant name + rule + what you said. Text to Jerel end of day.
